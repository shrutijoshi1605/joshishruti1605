Background

My Chapter

I spent years in India working across three very different environments. I started as a Technical Consultant in New Delhi, moved into a national defence programme where I gathered requirements from Indian Navy personnel and turned them into developer-ready specifications, and then spent six years at TCS where I mapped processes, configured enterprise platforms, and kept systems running for thousands of users across Asia Pacific.

What those years gave me is hard to put in a job title. I learned to sit with people who could not always explain what they needed and help them figure it out. I learned to ask the right questions, separate what someone says they want from what they actually need, and write it up clearly enough for a development team to build from. I also learned to keep three government bodies aligned across a six year project when they did not always agree.

In 2025 I made a deliberate decision. I left a settled career, moved to New Zealand with my family, and completed a Master of Professional Business Analysis at Victoria University of Wellington, passing with Merit in July 2026. Since arriving I have tutored Business and Systems Analysis at VUW, volunteered at Dress for Success Wellington where I mapped and redesigned their processes, and delivered real BA project work for New Zealand organisations including Cogo and Malagan Institute.

I am building my NZ career from the ground up, with everything I have learned, and I am genuinely ready to contribute.
